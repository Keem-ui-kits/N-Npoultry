## Register

## Users

Households, restaurants, and retailers around Machakos, Kenya. They arrive either searching directly for fresh eggs or via referral. Decision-making is fast — they want trust and a clear path to order.

## Product Purpose

N&N Poultry Palace is a farm-to-doorstep egg delivery brand. Eggs collected at 2 PM, packed by 5 PM, delivered before noon the next day. The site exists to convert visitors into WhatsApp orders or product-page browsers.

## Brand Personality

Envisioned, trustworthy, local. A farm that has vision and conviction — not a scrappy operation, not a corporate chain. Community-rooted with genuine ambition.

## Anti-references

Generic supermarket egg packaging aesthetics. Cheap food-delivery app UIs. Anything that looks like a template or an AI-generated landing page.

## Design Principles

1. Urgency without pressure — the freshness timeline is a feature, not a marketing trick.
2. Farm credibility — real place, real process, real people. Never abstract.
3. Space earns drama — let the dark background and imagery breathe.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Ensure contrast on gold-over-dark text.
